A Sky of One Person

— Observed from Menlo Park · 37.4°N 122.2°W —
Sub Coelo PatuliEquatorial aspect
Mag. limit: VI.5
Field: c. 30°
Drawn in ink, no apology

The subject, one J. Homer of the southern firmament of the Bay, having mapped neither stars nor seas of consequence, presents instead the small constellations he can claim: works shipped after dinner, day-labour rendered for the Corps, trade plied for the merchants of his town.
