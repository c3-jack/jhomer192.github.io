Opera Minora — Twelve stars, one figure

Twelve small constellations, charted between dinner and bedtime, ordered by magnitude. Songrank and WikiGame sit at the bright end — α and β — because they are the two that ship the most living traffic. The rest are tools, toys, experiments, and one harness (Claude Bot) that built most of the rest. Click any star to read what it is and where to find it.

How to read this

A filled disc means the project is live and open to the public. An open ring means source-only — the code is on GitHub but the demo isn't worth visiting yet. Dashed conjunction lines mark lineage: Claude Bot built nearly every other star here, so a faint blue line tethers it to WikiGame where the lineage is most visible.

What's missing

Earlier drafts of this site included a constellation called Sagitta Quaerens — travel-search projects (flight, hotel, apartment). They were removed in June 2026: each one was a demo-mode stub that loaded but never returned real data. A chart that admits "deployed" without admitting "works" is just a CV with extra steps. The travel projects will return when they actually do something.
